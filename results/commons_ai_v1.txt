AI images from Wikimedia Commons per generator: a free test of generators we never trained on (1 Nov 2026)

Data (scripts/download_commons_ai.py): 1,098 files from 21 Commons categories "Images generated by <tool>" (with their
subcategories); licence CC0, public domain or CC BY without SA only; JPEG/PNG/WebP, at least 0.25 MP; files up to 2048 px
as uploaded, bigger ones as Commons' 2048-px copy. Every file's author, licence and link: data/attribution/commons_ai_v1.csv.
Most are illustrations. The photo-like ones (they would pass as a photo at a glance) were picked by eye from contact
sheets: 202 (column photo_like). By their titles, 31 of these were not made from a prompt but are AI restorations or
edits of REAL photos (colourised 1900s portraits, "upscaled", "AI Enhanced", "camera deleted", 1930s town photos;
column kind). Evaluation only, never trained on.
