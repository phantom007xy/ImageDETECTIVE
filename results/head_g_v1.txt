== 2. Found: head F flags real close-up faces (a "close-up face = AI" shortcut) ==
Real close-ups (scripts/make_real_face_crops.py: SAM 3 faces of at least 200 px, square crops of 5-6.5 eye distances,
from DEV photos: 129) set head F's fair line above (4.7%, the strictest pool). The app's full verdict at its own lines
(python -m src.eval.full_verdicts --list features/lists/real_face_crops_dev.csv):
  real close-ups of Commons portraits 77: 11.7% called AI (1 "AI-generated") | Commons people 8: 25% | phone 1: 1 of 1
  real close-ups of OpenImages photos 38: 0%       | all 129: 12 called AI (9.3%) - over the 5% budget
Likely cause: head F's AI class has 1,200 tight Stable Diffusion face images, its real class almost no tight real faces.
Whole photos never showed it (polished dev 4.8%, my phone photos 0%): it appears when a real portrait is cropped tight,
like a profile picture. Adding real close-ups to training (G1r) already lowers it and raises catches at the same line.
Next: more real close-ups (OpenImages SID files 15-29; Commons v4 portraits and selfies, CC0/CC BY), GAN faces (SFHQ),
and the close-ups as a calibration pool.
