Edit check with person edits: a new head on the frozen fine-tuned PE (EC3), 1 Nov 2026

Why: on my selfies the frozen EC3 features separate Muse edits from their originals almost perfectly (AUC 0.99), but
the EC3 head scored every photo with a person far lower, so local edits of a person stayed under the line (10-12%;
results/self_edits_v1.txt). Suggested and tried first: keep the PE frozen and retrain only the head with person edits.
Data: the 22,307 training pictures of EC3 (one random training view each, src/features/extract_pe.py --mode train
--pe-weights weights/edit_check_v3/pe_EC3.pt) + Muse edits of my 30 selfies (1 Nov I allowed them for training:
the 60 test edits with the "person" prompts and 89 new ones with the separate "person_train" prompts; 149 edits,
4 training views each; private, totals only).
Fair test (python -m src.eval.edit_head_cv ...): 3 folds by ORIGINAL selfie; each fold's selfies (original and all
their edits, in the app's view) are held out once. The edit line is set exactly as in the app: jointly with head F on
the six public real pools, the joint trade, the edit check alone at most 0.5% of every pool. Averages over the folds.

-> A head learned from zero loses EC3's strength on people-free edits (49% / 59% without selfies): EC3's own head was
   trained together with the PE. Starting from EC3's head and pulling the new head back towards it (an L2 pull to the
   start, 0.1) keeps that strength (75.2% / 74.5% vs 76.5% / 74.2%) AND learns person edits: my held-out local edits
   12% -> 86%, whole edits 84% -> 98%, none of my held-out real selfies flagged.
Chosen: from EC3's head, pull 0.1, selfie pairs x5, now fitted on all 30 selfies -> weights/edit_check_v3/head_EC3p.pt
(private; the PE is unchanged, so its features of my private photos stay valid). The app uses it after calibrate_app.
Caveats: the held-out selfies are me, from the same sessions as the training selfies (optimistic); the honest test is
new selfies (other place, clothes, light). Other people's faces stay unmeasured: I may edit no one else.
