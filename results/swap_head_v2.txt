Face-swap head v2: OpenForensics (face swaps in still photos) next to DFDC (1 Nov 2026)

Why: the face-swap check (results/dfdc_face_v1.txt) learned only from DFDC video frames. On photos whose faces were
replaced by AI-made faces it was blind: OpenForensics Test-Dev AUC 0.63, 0.4% of the replaced faces caught at its
strict line (0.6% of the photos).

Data (scripts/download_openforensics.py, src/features/extract_openforensics_faces.py; photos stay on the laptop):
  OpenForensics (Le et al., ICCV 2021; Zenodo 5528418; CC BY 4.0): Open Images photos in which some faces were replaced
  by GAN-made faces and blended in; the other faces stay real, so real and fake faces share camera, light and
  compression. Every face has a box and a label; SAM 3 finds the faces with the app's settings and each SAM face takes
  the label of the annotated box it overlaps (IoU >= 0.3, one to one; unmatched SAM faces are skipped).
  - Val split, all 7,308 photos: 4,441 real / 10,380 replaced faces kept (428 too small), 4 random training crops each.
    85% of the photos train, 15% (by photo) are the dev part.
  - Test-Dev split, a random 1,500-photo sample, app crop: 1,531 real / 2,270 replaced faces. Never used for a choice.
  DINOv3 features saved from the last layer (what the app's heads read) and after blocks 8, 12 and 16 (same pass).
  Training mixes: OF = OpenForensics train part; DFDC = DFDC parts 0-2 (as in v1); SID real = real faces of SID-Set
  train files 0-9 (OpenImages), minus every photo in a real test pool (290 unseen OpenImages photos and the Muse pairs'
  real photos come from the same files).

Comparison (python -m src.eval.swap_head_v2): every head gets its own strict line, as in the app: at most 0.5% of the
real faces of every public real pool (polished Commons, OpenImages val and unseen, Commons v3 dev, real close-ups,
selfies+portraits, SID val, DFDC part 3 real) above it. My phone faces can raise it further when calibrate_app runs.

  Findings:
  - OpenForensics alone is learned well (AUC 0.98) but does not carry over to video swaps; DFDC adds that.
  - The strict line, not the AUC, decides what the app catches: a few real faces that look "swapped" set it. More real
    faces in training (SID real) moved the line from +5.52 to +3.64 and multiplied the swaps caught by ~6.
  -> Face-swap head v2 = DFDC + OF + SID real faces, last layer: weights/swap_v2/head_swap_v2.pt (private; DFDC rule:
     never published). calibrate_app now uses it and also reports the OpenForensics test swaps.

  Middle layers separate photo face swaps far better (5% line: 6% -> 74-85%), but single real faces still set the
  strict line; with real faces from more sources in training (as in part 1) this should move. WildDeepfake stays low
  for every OpenForensics-only head (AUC 0.60-0.68): video swaps need DFDC in the mix.
  Next: DFDC + OF + SID real on middle layers, compared with v2 at the strict line. If it wins, the private test must
  save middle-layer face features once (src/eval/private_photo_test.py does it now) before calibrate_app.

Caveats: OpenForensics swaps are GAN faces blended into photos (2021 methods); today's face-swap apps may leave other
traces. The Test-Dev number is a sample of 1,500 photos from one dataset.
