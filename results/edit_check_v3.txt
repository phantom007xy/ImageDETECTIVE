Muse-edit check v3: Meta's PE-Core-L/14, fully fine-tuned (night of 31 Oct -> 1 Nov 2026)

What: all 303,507,456 PE weights + the linear head over 6 layers x 1,024 (src/models/pe_core.py) trained together,
starting from the frozen PE + head_EC_both (results/edit_check_v1.txt). src/models/tune_pe.py: batch 16, 7,000 steps
(about 5 passes), AdamW, lr 2e-5 for the last block with layer-wise decay 0.85 (3.4e-7 for the patch embedding),
head lr 1e-4, decoupled pull towards the pretrained weights, stochastic depth 0.1, warmup 300 + cosine, bf16 +
gradient checkpointing; laptop RTX 5050, ~1.95 s/step (~3.8 h). Log: weights/pe_ft_v1_log.txt (private).
Training: 22,307 pictures, 14,878 real (the edits' originals, Commons train + v3 train, SID-Set OpenImages files
10-29, pair reals) and 7,429 rendered by Muse (training edits of people-free photos + Muse pair fakes). No dev or
test photo in training (src/eval/leak_check.py, also checks the photos each edit was made from).

== Curve (dev sets; joint line with head F, without the trade) ==
Training loss 0.63 (step 100) -> 0.25 (3,000) -> 0.16 (7,000). Dev AUC originals v2 vs edits v2: 0.970 (step 0)
-> 0.980 (2,000) -> 0.989 (4,000) -> 0.986-0.988 (4,500-7,000): flat from ~4,000. Rule fixed beforehand: 10,000 steps
only if still rising at 7,000 -> not run.

Why the strict line first hid the progress: the fine-tuned model gives ONE real photo (a retouched studio portrait in
the polished dev set) an edit logit of about +13. Without the trade that photo sets the line (+13.3) and local edits
stay at 16-17%. With the joint trade (src/calibrate/calibrate_edit_check.py --give-up 1: head F gives up its lowest
flag in its tightest pool, polished dev; its AI line -0.30 -> -0.17) the edit check may flag that one photo instead,
and its line drops to +3.87. False alarms per pool are unchanged (the budget is moved, not widened).
