Reading:
  1. Path B finds about 1 in 4 SID-Set-style edits (a small AI patch pasted into an untouched photo) at a
     strict line, with a rough region (IoU ~0.2). Weak, but a real signal.
  2. It finds NO Muse edits: Muse re-renders the whole photo (results/muse_edits_v1.txt), so there is no
     "edited inside vs. camera-pixel outside" contrast to find. Muse edits are Path A's job (head G).

     Path A). Local pasted-in AI edits (e.g. generative fill that keeps the rest untouched) are "limited",
