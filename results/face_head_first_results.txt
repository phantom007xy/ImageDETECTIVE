First face head (Path F), 30 Oct 2026. Frozen DINOv3-L, linear head on the average patch vector of each face crop.
Faces: SAM 3 ("face", score >= 0.5, >= 64 px), crop rule from results/face_crop_calibration.txt.
Training: SID-Set train files 0-9 (real = OpenImages, AI = FLUX), 4 random copies per face (k 2.6-4.0).
Tests: the fixed app crop (k = 4.0, JPEG 90). WildDeepfake: its own ready-made crops ("measured on tighter crops").
All numbers are per face unless stated otherwise.

fake: 410 sequences | Spearman(score, sharpness) +0.01 | Spearman(score, brightness) -0.53

-> Not blur. Brightness: darker faces get higher AI scores. WildDeepfake fakes are brighter (median 96 vs. 78),
   which pushes the AUC below 0.5. The head has never seen a face swap, so no swap signal was expected.
