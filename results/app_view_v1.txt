App view (size fix), 31 Oct 2026: how big does the app show a photo to DINOv3?

Why: training shows square crops of 60-100% of the short side, resized to 448 x 448. The app showed the whole
photo with the LONG side at 448 ("long448"), so a 4:3 photo was 1.33x and a 16:9 photo 1.78x more zoomed out
than anything the head saw in training. "short448" = short side 448, long side at most 896 (the training
scale; up to 2x the patches, so up to 2x the DINOv3 time). Square images look the same in both views.
