Muse Image same-content pairs (v1) and the wider brightness range, 30 Oct 2026. Frozen DINOv3-L + linear head.

== Data ==
500 real photos picked from SID-Set train files 0-9 (OpenImages; 300 with a SAM face), split 400 train / 100 val
before generating. Muse Spark (muse-spark-1.3, standard mode, reasoning_effort minimal) captioned them:
403 ok, 97 skipped (children or sensitive), $1.14. Muse Image (search tools off): 396 fakes, 7 refused, $3.96.
Each fake is centre-cropped to its real photo's aspect ratio and resized to its size (scripts/make_pair_lists.py).
-> 320 train pairs (16 random copies each), 76 val pairs. SID-Set files 0-9 are left out of the heads below, so
no val real photo was seen in training.
Brightness range in training: x0.85-1.15 (old) -> x0.6-1.5 (new, "aug_v2"), the same for real and AI.

== Conclusions ==
-> Muse pairs fix the Muse problem: held-out Muse test 43% -> 95% caught (81% at a strict threshold).
-> But C flags 24% of real Chameleon photos, 31% of those with faces: it partly learned "polished people photo
   = AI". Our real training photos are older amateur Flickr snapshots (OpenImages); Muse fakes, Unsplash photos
   (Chameleon's reals) and modern phone photos all look polished. C is NOT safe for a judge's real selfie.
-> The wider brightness range barely changes the darkness bias (logit rise at half brightness: A +0.42,
   B +0.43, C +0.40); only the bright end improves a little. Real low-light photos must be tested.
-> Next: modern, polished real photos in training (licence-clean), paired with Muse fakes of the same content.
