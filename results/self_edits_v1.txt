Why (python -m src.eval.edit_pairs_check --list features/lists/muse_self_edits_v1.csv, run by me):
  local edits (40): changed area median 5.1% (people-free photos: 17%); fine detail outside the change correlates 0.34
    with the original (plain resize 0.98) -> Muse re-renders the whole selfie, it does not copy my pixels back.
  edit check, whole picture: originals median -21.3, local edits -2.3 (line +3.87) -> AUC 0.99: it sees the edits but
    scores my real selfies (and so their edits) far lower than people-free photos (real people-free photos ~ -10..-14).
  edit check on the face crop: AUC 0.85; head F on the face: 0.62; face-swap head: 0.43 (no help).
  whole edits (20): edit check AUC 1.00, 85% above the line; head F AUC 1.00.
-> Likely cause (a guess that fits the numbers): every training edit was a people-free photo and every training photo
   with people was real, so the check learned "a person in the picture -> real". Fix needs Muse edits of a person in
   training; the only person I may edit is myself (plan Section 0).
A line only for photos with a face would have to stay above +0.42 (a real portrait in the polished dev set), i.e. it
would catch roughly a quarter of these edits: not enough.
