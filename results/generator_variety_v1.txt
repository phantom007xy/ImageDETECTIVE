Generator variety, step 1 (30 Oct 2026): SD 1.5 / 2.1 faces in training, SDXL held out completely.
Frozen DINOv3-L + linear head. Dev sets only; own threshold per head (at most 5% false alarms on every
real dev pool); app preprocessing (src.features.extract --mode app).

== Data ==
1,200 SD faces for training (600 SD 1.5, 600 SD 2.1; python scripts/make_eval_lists.py --sd-faces ...
--sd-faces-train 600), none of them in the dev list features/lists/sd_faces.csv; no SDXL at all.
Kept small on purpose: far more AI portraits than real portraits would teach "portrait = AI".
E = D (results/commons_v1_heads.txt) + these 1,200 faces (4 copies each).

== Conclusions ==
-> Generator variety works: with 1,200 SD 1.5/2.1 faces, SDXL faces (a generator never seen) are caught 92%
   instead of 37%, while Muse stays at 93%.
-> Caveat: the SDXL faces come from the same dataset as the training faces (same creator, retouching and
   framing), so part of the gain may be "knows this dataset's style". Step 2 (other open models, our own
   prompts) and the fresh Day 8 test will show how far it carries.
