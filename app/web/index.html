About ImageDETECTIVE

ImageDETECTIVE is an image-authenticity investigation tool. It combines visual detectors, available file metadata and optional AI-assisted explanations to help people assess signs of AI generation or manipulation.

An investigation provides evidence, not proof. "Likely authentic" does not establish who took an image, where it was taken or whether its caption is true.

How the investigation works

One upload runs every check on this app's server. The verdict comes only from ImageDETECTIVE's own detectors. For pictures that are not clearly authentic, Meta's investigator then points to the most suspicious object and writes a short explanation from the detectors' numbers; it never decides the verdict.

How the verdict is set

The thresholds are set on development photos so that at most 5% of real photos get an AI verdict. When the checks don't point clearly either way, the answer is "Uncertain" instead of a guess. The percentage shown is the whole-image check's calibrated AI evidence; the other checks are shown as findings, not as probabilities.

How to read the evidence colours

  • Red: parts of the picture whose signals point to AI generation or manipulation.
  • Pale green: parts whose signals point to a real photo.
  • Dark blue: weak or mixed signals.
  • Numbered boxes mark faces (red: looks AI-made or swapped; orange: some signs; green: no clear signs; grey: too small to check). A yellow outline, when shown, is the object Meta's investigator pointed to.

Known limitations

  • It can't tell when or where a photo was taken: a real photo forwarded with a false story still looks real.
  • Classic manual edits (old-style Photoshop cut-and-paste) are not covered; face swaps and small AI edits only partly.
  • Some newer image generators are caught less often once a picture has lost its file label (for example in a screenshot).
  • Built for photos: drawings, documents, chat screenshots and videos are out of scope.

What happens to your upload

  • The image is checked on this app's server and kept in memory for at most 30 minutes (for the investigator step); it is never saved to disk.
  • For images that are not clearly authentic, a copy at most 1,024 pixels long is sent to Meta's Model API in standard mode (Meta doesn't train on it). The explanation is written from numbers only.
  • "Try to fool it" sends only your text prompt to Meta's API.
